CutClip

CutClip is a desktop app for content creators. It watches live streams, detects the most exciting moments, turns them into short vertical clips (1080x1920) and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

  • Runs locally on the creator's Windows computer.
  • Creators connect their accounts through each platform's official login.
  • Every post can be reviewed, scheduled or cancelled before publishing.
