CutClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

CutClip is a desktop app that runs locally on the user's computer. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal data.

2. Data we access

When you connect a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, CutClip receives, through the official login of each platform:

  • Basic profile information (such as display name, avatar and account ID), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens, used only to publish videos you have chosen to publish and to read basic stats of those videos.

3. How data is stored

Tokens and app data are stored only on your own computer. Credentials are protected with the operating system's encryption where available. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

4. How data is used

Data is used exclusively to upload and schedule your clips to your own accounts and to display their performance inside the app. We do not use your data for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect an account at any time in the app, which deletes its stored tokens. You can also revoke CutClip's access in the settings of each platform. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all local data.

6. Children

CutClip is not intended for users under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
